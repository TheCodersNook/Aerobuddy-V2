ANALYTIC HAND-CALCS ONLY -- NOT FEA. PETG Z-strength 25 MPa assumed;
allowables: 12 MPa peak, 8 MPa cyclic (knockdowns for print variance).

Check                                        Stress   Allow  Margin
Hinge pin shear (15 N yank)                   3.12 MPa  12.0 MPa    3.8x
Knuckle bearing on pin                        1.71 MPa  12.0 MPa    7.0x
Knuckle root bending (15 N yank)             11.11 MPa  12.0 MPa    1.1x
Press-cap wall compression (25 N)             0.33 MPa   8.0 MPa   24.5x
Can-bore wall, off-axis press                 0.05 MPa   8.0 MPa  160.0x
Faceplate pocket floor, button 5 N            3.83 MPa   8.0 MPa    2.1x
Nozzle-block screw shear (25 N / 2)           6.22 MPa  12.0 MPa    1.9x
Nozzle-block screw pull-out                   0.50 MPa   8.0 MPa   16.1x

Weakest link: knuckle root bending, 1.1x at a 15 N yank -- but that
is a worst-case yank; at a realistic 5 N fold force the margin is
3.2x. Next thinnest: nozzle-block screw shear (1.9x at 25 N fire
force; conservative -- the block also bears on the pocket floor)
and faceplate pocket floor (2.1x). All other margins >3.8x.
Press path margins are otherwise >10x: the can valve spring (25 N)
is small against PETG section sizes. The real risks are FATIGUE
and CREEP, not one-off yield -- hence PETG (not PLA) and the
1,000-fold + 100-press acceptance tests in the build notes.
